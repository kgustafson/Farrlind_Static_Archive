Session 25: The Fight in the Ice Dome

Canon Summary

  • Physical date: 2026-08-17

  • Ending location: Ice Cave

  • Review status: applied, applied 2026-08-17

The session began with the party setting out for a dangerous exploration within The Gale, a region defined by harsh winds and snow connected to the elemental plane of air. They successfully navigated through thin ice to find an Ice Cave, a location marked by ancient wall carvings and symbolic imagery related to early inhabitants.

Inside the cave, the party found evidence of early communication, discovering infernal markings on the walls and floor, which Makani (Mikani) was able to interpret using their knowledge of infernalism.

Using levitation magic, the party gained better views of the terrain and investigated an ice wall, which led them to a hidden chamber containing a summoning circle. Further exploration revealed that this circle resembled one found on top of a tower, with central writing indicating "ice in Demonic," suggesting methods for elemental summoning.

Behind the ice wall, they discovered a route leading to this chamber. This area was immediately dangerous, as it led to an encounter involving an Ice Elemental, three Ice Mephits, and a Polar Serpent emerging from the wall.

Faban initiated combat against these entities. The party utilized powerful magic, including Shatter, to engage the Ice Elemental and associated methods. During this fight, they dealt with complex mechanics involving piercing and cold damage, and discussed the nature of elemental creatures and "Death's Door."

The confrontation escalated into a final sequence against multiple boss creatures, including the Polar Serpent and Salamanders. The combat involved physical grappling, damage interaction with ice formations.

During this intense struggle, the party engaged in complex role-playing math regarding spellcasting rules, dice rolls, and damage calculations, discussing the viability of various healing spells like Mass Healing Word and Revivify.

A critical moment involved a physical struggle where the Polar Serpent attempted to burrow and grapple a party member. A subsequent attack using a lightning bolt spell was aimed at the serpent, requiring Dexterity saving throws to determine if the salamander or serpent dispersed.

The session concluded with a dramatic sequence involving healing attempts, resurrection processes for Bridget and Corvinus, and a final confrontation that resulted in grim consequences. The party discussed the implications of returning souls and the physical state of the deceased. Mikani had a difficult choice to make. She revivified Corvinus. Bridget dies.

Following the combat, a giant named Rastov appeared, who guided the group through a cold, slippery area toward the Monastery of the Open Hand.
