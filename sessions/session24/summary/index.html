Session 24: Entering the Gale

Canon Summary

  • Physical date: 2025-07-12
  • In-game date: Session 24
  • Starting location: The Gale
  • Ending location: Ice Cave
  • Review status: applied, applied 2026-07-13

The session began with the party teleporting from Catur into The Gale, a region defined by torrentious winds and snow, which is fundamentally connected to the elemental plane of air. During this journey, Mikani communed with nature to determine the existence of a nearby settlement within the harsh environment. Roon tested his winged boots while in the volatile atmosphere and encountered a white dragon emerging from the snow.

The party enters the Gale following the map provided to them from the cartographer from the nearby settlement. They find one of the marked ice caves within The Gale marked on the map. They enter the ice cave within The Gale, which contained wall carvings depicting historical events and symbolic imagery related to early inhabitants who communicated through oral tradition rather than writing.

Further exploration revealed that the cavern had varying dimensions: most of the space was 20 feet high, but one section was domed at the top and extended up about 60 feet. During this time, a character named Rune passed away.

Later, while navigating the ice cave, the party discovered a spot of floor with thinner ice, suggesting a potential passage through the cavern. They discussed survival checks related to the cold environment and used sources like whiskey for warmth.

As they explored deeper into the cave system, they observed "the ghost" moving through the space, leading to discussions about perception checks and navigating the cavern. The party established a procedure for night watch, defining detection ranges of 30 feet.

The group then faced an immediate crisis: a cave-in occurred, with cracks appearing on the ceiling, trapping them in the ice cave. They discussed using water/ice manipulation, specifically dimension door magic, as a potential escape method.
