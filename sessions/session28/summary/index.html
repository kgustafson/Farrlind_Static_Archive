Session 28: Secrets at the Monastary

Canon Summary

  • Physical date: 2026-09-21
  • In-game date: 1832 AS --- Namal 28
  • Starting location: Monastery of the Open Hand
  • Ending location: Monastery of the Open Hand
  • Key locations: Monastary of the Open Hand
  • Review status: applied, applied 2026-09-21

During the investigation, the party found the captive's short sword and short bow, which were determined to be common, non-magical weaponry.

The captive was contacted through a proxy, identified as a "demon chick" or tiefling, and the party noted the captive's pale skin, black eyes, and baldness, speculating he resembled a mannequin in human skin. The Drow had also been observed attacking with a shadowy sword, which vanished upon the captive's death.

The party then proceeded to search the defeated creatures, including a minotaur, an orc, and a goliath, finding various coins and runic coin pouches. They also found a magical pouch belonging to the elf, which was identified as Silvertone from the College of Eloquence.

The party discussed the mechanics of the items found, including the runic markings on the pouches and the non-magical nature of the hilt and sword found among the captured group. They also discussed the potential dangers of the items, noting that putting the found pouch into their bag might cause a "black hole type of bad news."

The investigation led to the deduction that the person wielding the shadow blade was a Warlock with an undead or undying patron, marked by the symbol of the cult of elemental chaos. The party gathered information from Hystera, a level 20 wizard and pupil of the Warlock, who provided context regarding the encounter and the monastery.

They learned that the Wells include Ardor, Nibane, Syspeed, Korok, Goosan, and Omnium, and that the books contain a catalog detailing their creation and cosmology. They learned that the Wand of Wells was created by four gods—Shaar (order), Chaotix (chaos), Evil (elements), and Death (underworld)—and was expelled with Chaotix, leading to the elemental cataclysm.

The party learned that the Wand acted as the arbiter of arcana and connected to the Wells, and that the only viable path forward was to face the elemental cataclysm. They also learned that the Outer Rim is a chaotic area between the mortal realm and the Celestial Isles, and that the path to the Celestial Isles sits upon the Outer Rim.

The group discussed the threat posed by Lord Salazar, the demon lord of the lightning, who is believed to be manipulating the cults of elemental chaos. They concluded that the only way to reestablish order among the Wells is to possess the Wand of Wells, though its exact location remained unknown.

During this ritual, they learned that the Wand is not made of anything tangible and chooses who interacts with it. The session concluded with a lore presentation detailing the wand's history and the party experiencing a final sensory encounter: the sounds of wings beating and an avalanche approaching, while observing a white dragon.
