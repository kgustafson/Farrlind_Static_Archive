Session 26: The Monastery of the Open Hand

Canon Summary

  • Physical date: 2026-08-22
  • In-game date: 1832 AS — Namal 27
  • Starting location: The Gale
  • Ending location: Monastery of the Open Hand
  • Key locations: Monastary of the Open Hand
  • Major outcome: Buried Bridget.
  • Review status: applied, applied 2026-08-23

The session began with the party reflecting on previous events, including finding sigil markings in an ice cave and fighting frost-ice themed creatures. They were then guided by a kindly giant named Rassel toward the Monastery of the Open Hand. Upon arrival, they observed that the monks were cautious strangers, seeking solace and avoiding conflict.

The party negotiated with the monastery gate, where they encountered Rasolf, who offered bread. They proceeded to knock on the door and were admitted by Histata, a gnome monk who served as the keeper of the monastery since its founding approximately 957 years ago. Histata provided historical context regarding the monastery's history, detailing the conflict between frost giants and dragonkin (Hesterahango) during the Million Years' War. Histata summoned her son, Ipipip, to guide us to the burial grounds for the burying of Bridgit.

Along the way, the party observed the symbols in the great hall and it detailed the history of monestary, including treatises on monastic tradition and histories, which detailed the five tenets of the monastery, known as the tenets of the open hands. They noted that discerning symbols and magic was difficult. Faban was able to read the old common tongue and translate for Mikani the text.

The party proceeded to the graveyard where they encountered Rayetta, a Tiefling monk. They discussed ritual magic, specifically the "funeral right" spell, which prevents undeath for seven days unless a wish is made, noting that casting it takes extra time.

The group then performed funeral rites for Bridget, involving singing a lament and invoking prayers. The party realized they never asked Bridget about her beliefs and what god we should pray to. We discussed if Faban should ask the head monk about the reliefs in the hall and whether to handle these inquiries privately or as a party. Faban sang his lament in a voice that even surpassed any singing anyone had heard.

To ensure protection against evil, the party performed a ritual over Bridget's body using divine power. Afterward, Samuel, a half-elvish man, appeared and warned Hisata about an impending attack. Hisata then told Rayetta to take the party to safety. Which they found in the monastery dormitories where the party long rested.

Later, the party traveled through the monastery, where they encountered Hysteria, who was speaking with Samuel in an office area. They discussed murals and history, and a subtle encounter involving psychic static/pressure occurred when passing by Hysteria, leading to a discussion about protective magic and artifacts.

The group then sought knowledge in the library, where they met Bedrock, the librarian, who possessed extensive knowledge of ancient texts and the wells. They learned about planar disruption that caused The Gale and the formation of the Celestial Isles through volcanic activity and elemental gods like Ederl.

Further lore revealed that demons have been allies since the Billion Years' War, and a white dragon attacked them in The Gale. Saifi advised the group to talk to Balrog regarding the Well, which is located in Balrog. The party also learned about an artifact held by Hysteria that generates psychic background radiation, restricting spellcasting.

The session concluded with the party finding ancient texts and learning about the Celestial language, historical texts on the Rim Islands, and the formation of the Celestial Isles through volcanic activity and elemental gods. They borrowed the Tome of Ice Nasties (Volume 3) from Bedrock. The session ended with a directive to return next week to meet with Samuel.
