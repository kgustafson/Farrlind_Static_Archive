Session 30: Beginning Trip to Hanidal

Canon Summary

  • Physical date: 2026-10-11
  • In-game date: 1832 AS --- Hephal 02
  • Starting location: Monastery of the Open Hand
  • Ending location: Outside the Gale
  • Key locations: Monastary of the Open Hand
  • Review status: applied, applied 2026-10-12

The session began with the party resting at a monastery, reflecting on the recent confrontation with a white dragon and engaging in deep philosophical discussions about justice, religion, and politics, referencing the goddess Namaloa.

The investigation led the party to seek geographical context, discussing the relationship between Hanadal, the Celestial Isles, and the Outer Rim. They learned that Hanadal is east of the Celestial Isles, and the region is characterized by volatile racial and religious conflicts.

The party then focused on the history of magic, discussing the origin of wizardry and the concept of "wells." They learned that island nations were among the first to use magic during the Age of Discovery, though this history is poorly documented due to destruction. They also discussed texts like the "Book of Wells" and the "Book of Many Colors," realizing that the destruction of history suggests powerful magic was deliberately erased.

Later, the party sought information in the monastery's Library, where Rune, Corvinus, and Gildas searched for historical texts. They discussed the nature of ghosts, aberrations, and various factions coexisting in the magical environment.

The journey planning involved discussing travel options, focusing on finding a boat and a route to Hanadal via Morak. They learned about the island of Honeydoll and the well within it.

They discussed the necessity of survival, the risk involved, and the need to move quickly.

After the journey, the party settled into a camp, sharing stories around a campfire. Later, a dream sequence occurred involving Bridget and Kornos, where Bridget revealed that Salazar had captured her, and Corvinus was associated with an infernal mark on his head.

The session culminated in a dramatic event where the party found themselves in a resplendent library surrounded by angelic creatures. They discovered a path to a large double door bearing the symbol of Seah, leading to a confrontation with a mysterious woman who claimed possession of an item.

During this encounter, the party experienced a dramatic transition involving the Wand of Wells, followed by waking up back into their own bodies, leaving them with unresolved questions about the item's origin and the nature of the "permanent calamity."
