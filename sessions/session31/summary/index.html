Session 31: Buying Trinkets in Morak

Canon Summary

  • Physical date: 2026-10-26
  • In-game date: 1832 AS --- Hephal 03
  • Starting location: Morak
  • Ending location: Morak
  • Key locations: Morak
  • Review status: applied, applied 2026-10-26

The session begins with the party resting at the Monastery of the Open Hand, engaging in philosophical discussions about justice, religion, and the goddess Namaloa. Their investigation leads them to seek geographical context regarding Hanadal, the Celestial Isles, and the Outer Rim, planning a journey to find passage.

The party then traveled along major trade routes, heading toward the port town of Morek. While traveling, they encountered a traveling merchant who provided context about the history and trade routes of Morek. The party discussed the possibility of acquiring a conveyance, such as a ship or ocean carriage, and sought information on necessary items like armor.

They entered a shop where a trinket salesman was selling magical items, leading to discussions about the properties and rarity of various amulets and rings.

Later, the party focused on acquiring magical items, discussing the cost of the Crystalline Ring and the Copper Ring, and the potential value of the Ring of Regeneration. They also discussed the power of the Apollo Amulet (Amulet of Bardic Acuity) and its effect on spellcasting.

The journey continued, involving negotiations for passage and a ship, where the party was directed toward a specific route and advised on bureaucratic processes. They encountered a stout dwarf near a shipwreck and interacted with the Harbor Master, who provided crucial information about the location and the ship.

Further travel led them to the Docks, where they observed activity and gathered new equipment. They also encountered a fishmonger who explained the properties and origin of dragon fish, noting they come from the Celestial Isles.

The party then focused on acquiring armor, discussing the trade-in of chainmail for splint mail, and the costs associated with various armor types. They learned that trading chainmail for splint mail yielded 100 gold pieces.

While at the docks, the party observed a mysterious figure putting up posters, and they used the Arcane Eye spell to follow this individual. They also found tomes related to Chaotix inside a barrel and observed a crate branded with the symbol of the Cult of Elemental Chaos.

The party gathered information about a poster referencing the "Sailor of the Giggling Heart" and the location Hanadal, learning that the area is volatile and associated with pirate threats. They received dragon fruit and sushi as a gift.

Finally, the party sailed away, discussing the high price of an artifact sold by a sailor, which granted free access into the Celestial Isles, and the mystery surrounding the symbol on the ring.
