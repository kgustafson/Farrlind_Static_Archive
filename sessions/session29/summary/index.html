Session 29: Fighting the White Beast

Canon Summary

  • Physical date: 2026-09-27
  • In-game date: 1832 AS --- Namal 29
  • Starting location: Monastery of the Open Hand
  • Ending location: Monastery of the Open Hand (within The Gale)
  • Key locations: Monastary of the Open Hand
  • Review status: applied, applied 2026-09-28

The session began with the party preparing for a confrontation with a gargantuan white dragon and an unknown person, finding themselves in the Monastery where they heard sounds of avalanches and dragon wings. They used the spell Dimension Door with Rune to position themselves behind the dragon, initiating a tactical discussion about positioning and using cover.

The party engaged in complex mechanics, including discussions on movement, bonus actions, and damage calculations. During the fight, the dragon used frost breath, and the party successfully used Wall of Fire to attempt to capture both the dragon and its rider.

The combat sequence involved intense action, including a surprise round, the casting of spells like Cloud of Daggers and Hell's Rebuke, and a critical hit. The Warlock summoned a long sword made of "blackened icarus metal" and engaged the dragon.

A ritual was performed involving the "sacred flame" and the deity Namaloa, which was sent into the dragon, involving a Dexterity saving throw and resulting in damage calculation. The party also discussed the mechanics of mass healing and the limitations of healing touch.

They also found a contract signed with Salazar, placing the Warlock under his command, and discovered various items on the captive, including a golden gilded ring, an infernal rod, and an amulet.

Later, the party discussed the mechanics of Monk abilities, movement, and bonus actions, and debated the rules surrounding spell effects and combat rules. They also discussed the physical limitations and capabilities of the dragon and the warlock.

The session concluded with the party finding a rare, enchanted longsword, a golden gilded ring, an infernal rod, and an amulet, along with a pouch containing 25 platinum and 207 gold. They also gained a level up, reaching level 10.
